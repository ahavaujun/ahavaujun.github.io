**Yonsei Artificial Intelligence** - *Aug 2025*

<br>

**Presented:** *"RayZer: Self-supervised Large View Synthesis with Foundation Models"*

Delivered a comprehensive seminar on recent advances in 3D vision, focusing on RayZer—a novel self-supervised approach for large-scale view synthesis. Discussed the integration of foundation models with transformer architectures for neural rendering and explored implications for 3D reconstruction tasks.

<br>

**Key Topics:**
<br>
    - Self-supervised learning paradigms in 3D vision
<br>
    - Large-scale view synthesis techniques
<br>
    - Foundation model architectures for novel view generation
<br>
    - Transformer-based approaches in neural rendering

<br>

**Reference:** [RayZer: A Self-supervised Large View Synthesis Model (arXiv:2505.00702)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.00702)
